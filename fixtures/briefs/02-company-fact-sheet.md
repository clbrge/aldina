# Northwind Robotics — Company Fact Sheet

Northwind Robotics designs autonomous material-handling systems for warehouses and
distribution centers. Founded in 2017, the company serves logistics operators across
North America and Europe.

## At a glance
- **Founded:** 2017
- **Headquarters:** Boston, MA
- **Employees:** 340
- **Customers:** 120+ facilities
- **Units deployed:** 4,800
- **Funding raised:** $86M (Series C)

## Products
- **Drift AMR** — autonomous mobile robot for case and tote movement
- **Tower** — high-density vertical storage and retrieval
- **Conductor** — fleet orchestration software with WMS integration
