# How Northwind cut order cycle time by 38%
### Case study · Logistics

![](visual:client-logo)

## The challenge
Northwind's flagship distribution center was missing same-day cutoffs during peak.
Manual tote movement created bottlenecks no amount of overtime could fix.

## The solution
A fleet of 60 Drift AMRs orchestrated by Conductor, integrated with the existing WMS
over eight weeks — no slowdown to live operations during rollout.

## The results
- **−38%** order cycle time
- **+22%** units shipped per shift
- **7 months** payback period

> "We hit same-day cutoffs through peak for the first time in years."
> — Grace Liu, COO
